# Code of conduct

ZenLock is a small project, and everyone is welcome here.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, also when you disagree.
- Talk about the work, not about the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is not clear.
- Help people who are new to the project or to open source.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment or threats, in public or in private.
- Comments about a person's identity, body, background or beliefs.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual language or images.
- Continued disruption of issues or pull requests.

## What happens

The maintainer can edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules.

To report a problem, open a [private security report](https://github.com/dheekshithsnewjourney-crypto/zenlock/security/advisories/new) and say that it is about conduct. Every report is read and kept private.
